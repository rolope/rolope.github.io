How I work

The same process Larii was born from: understand first, then test, then build.

  1. We talk

    On WhatsApp or a video call: what you want to solve, and for whom.

  2. Fixed-scope proposal

    What's in, what's out, timeline and a fixed price. No surprises.

  3. Clickable prototype

    You try it on your phone before the final code is written.

  4. Build

    Progress you can see, with automated tests where it matters.

  5. Launch and support

    Published under your account, with a user guide and support to get going.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. After we talk I send a fixed-scope proposal with a fixed price, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Who owns the code?

You do. It's delivered to your repository and the app is published under your account.

Do I need servers or technical knowledge?

No. I use managed services such as Google's Firebase, set up in your name. Many small projects fit within their free tier.

What happens after launch?

A support period for adjustments is included. After that you can hire maintenance or continue on your own.

Do you work with clients outside Mexico?

Yes, in Spanish or English.
