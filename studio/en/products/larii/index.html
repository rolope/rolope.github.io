Larii · Home app

Larii remembers. You live.

An app to run the home as a family: cleaning, menus, shopping, maintenance, calendar and bills, with an assistant that sums up your day.

Message me on WhatsApp

Built for a client

Running a home shouldn't live in one person's head

Before

  • One person has to remember everything.
  • To-dos scattered across notebooks, chats and memory.
  • Bills, services and groceries slip through the cracks.

With Larii

  • Everyone sees what's due today and who's on it.
  • One place for the home, on everyone's phone.
  • Larii flags what's overdue before it becomes a problem.

Open the app and you know what's next

Larii sums up the day: pending cleaning, what's for lunch, overdue maintenance and bills, and what's missing from the pantry. Every line takes you straight to it.

Cleaning that organizes itself

Tasks by room and frequency, from daily to yearly, each with an owner. Checklists reset themselves every period and everyone sees their own.

From meal plan to grocery list in one tap

Plan the week by food group and send the ingredients straight to each store's shopping list.

Never run out of the basics again

Lists by store and an inventory of household basics: mark what ran out and it lands on the right list.

Household bills, no surprises

Recurring bills with due dates, who usually pays and history. Only people with permission can see them.

One calendar for the whole family

Events by person and category that repeat on their own, alongside bill and maintenance due dates.

Built for a real family

Everyone sees their part

Three roles: admin, family and household staff. Finances stay with the people who should see them.

Works offline

Installs like an app and keeps working without signal; it syncs when you're back.

No passwords

Sign in with an email link; inviting someone takes a minute.

Real privacy

Permissions are enforced on the server, not just on screen, and covered by automated tests.

How it was made

  1. Specification

    We pinned down the house rules: who sees what, how often each room gets cleaned, when a bill is due.

  2. Clickable prototype

    The family tried it on their phones with sample data before anything was built.

  3. The real app

    Built on Firebase under the client's own account, ready to install.
