From home equipment to a habit

For people training at home

  • A plan built for their goal: lose weight, tone up or gain strength.
  • Step-by-step guided routines with the equipment they already own.
  • What to eat to complement the workouts.
  • Visible progress: measurements, activity and achievements.

For Inova

  • A membership that goes with every equipment sale.
  • Customers who keep using the product after buying it.
  • A direct channel to its community: live classes and coaching.
